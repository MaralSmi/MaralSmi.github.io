A hybrid fuzzy MCGDM framework based on Entropy–DEMATEL and COPRAS for sustainable solar technology selection

This research develops a hybrid fuzzy multi-criteria decision-making framework for evaluating and selecting sustainable solar technologies. Its methodological contribution combines a novel integration of Entropy, DEMATEL, and COPRAS with triangular fuzzy numbers (TFNs) to account for uncertainty in the decision-making process.

Explore research details

Research objective

Support structured and transparent comparisons of sustainable solar technology alternatives.

Methodology & innovation

Novel Entropy–DEMATEL–COPRAS integration, extended using triangular fuzzy numbers.

Ongoing

Techno-economic assessment of solar PV systems with cryptocurrency mining loads

This research investigates the techno-economic feasibility of integrating solar photovoltaic (PV) systems with Bitcoin mining operations through a system dynamics approach. A comprehensive dynamic model has been developed in Vensim, incorporating interconnected variables related to solar energy generation, battery storage, Bitcoin mining dynamics, electricity markets, investment costs, and environmental sustainability.

Explore research details

The model is designed to capture feedback relationships and complex interactions among these components, providing a foundation for evaluating the long-term economic and environmental implications of solar-powered Bitcoin mining systems.
